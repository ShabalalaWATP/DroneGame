SITE GUARD

Drones are inbound on your facility. Place sensors to detect them and defences to stop them — undetected drones can't be targeted.

  • Every system is a SAPIENT node: sensors send detection reports to a fusion node, which tasks the defences. Open the SAPIENT panel to watch the messages and see your laydown.
  • Pick a structure from the bottom bar (or press 1–7), then click the ground to place it.
  • Press Start wave when you're ready. Kills and surviving waves earn credits.
  • Mix it up: no single defence handles every drone type, and the enemy gets harder to hit and jam every wave.

The same scenario seed and laydown give the same attack every time. The terrain seed changes the ground (hills, woods, power lines) without changing the attack.
